How it goes

The form is filled. The decision stays yours.

01

Paste a link

Any job link, or pick one from your feed. Werkbot finds the real application page behind it.

02

It fills the form

A hidden browser types, picks and uploads. When a field has no source, it stops and asks you.

03

You press Send

Read every answer, edit anything, then send. Nothing goes out before that click.

It never sends without you. It never guesses.

Work permits, salary, start dates and legal consents are asked, never assumed.
