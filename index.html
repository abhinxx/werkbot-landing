How it goes

Tell it who you are once. It does the rest.

01

It learns you

Drop in your CV and say what you want. Werkbot builds your profile, searches every source, and ranks each role with a reason it fits.

02

It applies in parallel

A grid of hidden browsers fills real application forms side by side with a tailored one-page CV. You watch, or step into any one.

03

It asks, then remembers

When it does not know, it asks instead of guessing. Your answer is saved, so the next form asks less. The more you use it, the less it needs you.

04

It reaches out and follows up

Next: it finds people at the company, drafts the note for you to approve, and moves every application through sent, interview and offer.

Automation that never sends without you and never guesses.

Work permits, salary, start dates and legal consents are asked, never assumed. Every sentence about you is checked against your own CV before it goes anywhere.
