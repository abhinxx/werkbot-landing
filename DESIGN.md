# Design direction (unslop)

- **Product.** Werkbot fills in job applications for a student or graduate in a hidden browser, asks before it guesses, and stops at a Send button that only the person presses.
- **Character.** Exact, warm, a little playful-technical.
- **World.** A workshop: the form on the desk, a tool that does the careful work and leaves the decision to you. Surfaces stay near-white with a faint cool tint (the real app's `#f7f7f9`), ink `#0b0b0f`, one accent `#2F5BEA`.
- **Moments.** (1) Landing: a coded application form being filled by the agent, one field highlighted with the note "Asks before it guesses", match ring on the job card. (2) Technology: one drawn orchestration diagram (input, planner, parallel browser agents, decision layer, you at the end). The pastel card colors from the app appear only inside these two moments and each color means a state: blue filling, pink needs you, green ready, yellow job card.
- **Signature.** The circular match ring from the app, drawn as a thin ink ring on a grey track. It appears on the job card and on every agent tile, nowhere else.

## Rules kept from the skill
Inter (the app's own font file, 400 to 600), no hue gradients, no glow, no emoji, no gradient text, no glass, no bento. 4px spacing grid. Black pill buttons, 12 to 16px card radii with nested radii. Hairline borders at 8% ink. One easing curve, one entrance on load, no looping motion, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.

All sample data in the pictures is generic and marked as an illustration. No customers, no user counts, no benchmark numbers anywhere.
